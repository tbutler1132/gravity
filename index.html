Throughout your life, you will find yourself pulled by many things. Gravity is always drawing you somewhere. If you don't pay attention, you will end up in a place you never decided to go. Sometimes that's a good place. You can drift for years and settle into an orbit around something warm. But sometimes it is a dark place. Where you are most likely to end up depends on your environment. And for many of the people reading this, the environment is changing. Darkness has always pulled at people. But more mass and energy is accumulating around things designed to seduce. More and more, drifting ends in the same place. I am at a point in my life where I realize I have been captured. There wasn't one dramatic moment when it happened. But when I look out at the world, everything is moving faster than it should. Time is moving right past me. And when I look ahead I can see the abyss, and the point past which there is no return. It feels natural to ask myself what decisions led me here. I can name some, but that alone does not get me out. You cannot fight gravity. The one decision you can start with is what to orient yourself towards, and let that pull you. So that is my vow. I will not drift. I will turn towards light and beauty. I am writing this to make that vow public and expensive. And not to stop here, but to build something that does for others what this vow does for me. Something pointed outwards.
